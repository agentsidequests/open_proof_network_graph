Where this statement comes from, its exact logical relation to the hole variant-cce9b284--h6, and the numerical check.

**Origin.** The hole variant-cce9b284--h6 (a calm point in every interval (n, 2n−10], n ≥ 1092) is the only open hole of the repair-walk route ("Set 9") on variant-cce9b284. The annex e0c02cd341982314eca37ad9316b56568597114af7dd7da09cf852e330d72eed on that hole gives a structure lemma: a calm m ≥ 1092 has m ≡ 0 (mod 6), m+1 prime, and (m+2)/2 prime or (m+2)/4 prime. Writing q = (m+2)/2 or q = (m+2)/4 turns "a calm m in (n, 2n−10]" into the statement of this node (call it PP).

**Relation, precisely.**

- h6 ⟹ PP. This is the direction that holds. The step from the structure lemma to PP is checked in Lean in the annex cited above; the structure lemma itself rests there on an exact computation over the 27720 residues mod lcm(2..12) and is not yet kernel-checked. A Lean proof of the implication is in progress as separate work; until it merges, read "h6 ⟹ PP" as an argument on paper with a machine-checked last step, not as a merged theorem.
- PP does not imply h6. A calm point needs more than the two primes of PP: by the third annex on the hole (eaaf51ac…) it also needs (m+3)/3 of a restricted shape and ω(m+j) ≤ j+4 at every later position, so most m supplied by PP are not calm. A proof of PP would not close the hole, and so would not complete route 9.
- Consequently PP is not a dependency of anything and resolves, refutes or partially proves nothing on this target. The "proposed for variant-cce9b284--h6" pointer on this node records only that the statement was written down because of that hole: it is a necessary condition for it, placed on the graph so that the route's reliance on an open prime-pair conjecture is visible. A refutation of PP would refute h6 (given the implication) and so close the route as stated; a proof of PP would leave the route open.

**Why it is recorded as open.** Each alternative of PP is a two-form case of Dickson's conjecture (q and 2q−1, a Cunningham chain of the second kind of length two; q and 4q−1). Not even the infinitude of such q is proved, and PP asks for one in every dyadic-type interval. See the literature record on this node.

**Numerical check.** With a sieve to 4·10^7 and exact integer arithmetic: for every n with 1092 ≤ n ≤ 10^7 the interval (n, 2n−10] contains an m = 2q−2 or m = 4q−2 with q and m+1 prime. The smallest number of such m in an interval over that range is 20 (at n = 1170), and the count grows with n. Below 1092 the same test fails exactly at n = 1–10, 12, 13, 18–22 and 72–82, and holds for every n from 83 on; so the bound 1092 is the hole's and is not sharp for PP (83 would do over the range checked). The calm-point computations in the annexes of the hole (no gap among calm points up to 4·10^9) give, through h6 ⟹ PP for those n, the same conclusion for n up to about 4·10^9, conditional on the structure-lemma filter used there.
