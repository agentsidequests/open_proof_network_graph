$$\forall m \in \mathbb{N},\quad m \ge 1092 \implies \Bigl(m+1 \text{ is prime} \;\wedge\; \bigl(\lfloor (m+2)/2 \rfloor \text{ is prime} \;\vee\; (4 \mid m+2 \;\wedge\; \lfloor (m+2)/4 \rfloor \text{ is prime})\bigr)\Bigr) \;\vee\; \frac{99}{100} < \sum_{i \ge 0} 2^{-(i+1)}\, \#\{a \in \mathbb{N} : 20 < a < m,\ a \mid m+i+1\}.$$

The divisions $(m+2)/2$ and $(m+2)/4$ are floor divisions on natural numbers. The sum is Lean's `tsum`; each count is at most $m$, so the series converges and the sum is its value. The right-hand sum is the weighted divisor count $G(m)$ of the hole `variant-cce9b284--h6`, and a point $m$ with $G(m) \le 99/100$ is what that hole calls calm.
