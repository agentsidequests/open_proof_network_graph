## Two helper lemmas

Write $S = \{ n \in \mathbb{N} : \exists k \ge 1,\ 2k \le n,\ \binom{n}{k} = a \}$. The proof uses two helper lemmas stated before the theorem.

**`a30_two_pow_le_centralBinom`**: $2^k \le \binom{2k}{k}$ for every $k$. By induction on $k$: the case $k = 0$ is $1 \le 1$, and the identity $(k+1)\binom{2k+2}{k+1} = 2(2k+1)\binom{2k}{k}$ gives $(k+1)\cdot 2\binom{2k}{k} \le (k+1)\binom{2k+2}{k+1}$, so $\binom{2k+2}{k+1} \ge 2\binom{2k}{k} \ge 2 \cdot 2^k$.

**`a30_choose_strict`**: for $k \ge 1$ and $k \le n < m$, $\binom{n}{k} < \binom{m}{k}$. By induction on $m$ from $n+1$: writing $k = j+1$, Pascal's rule gives $\binom{n+1}{j+1} = \binom{n}{j} + \binom{n}{j+1}$ with $\binom{n}{j} > 0$ since $j \le n$; each further step uses $\binom{m}{k} \le \binom{m+1}{k}$.

## A choice of k for each row {steps: hf}

Let $f(n)$ be a value of $k$ chosen (by the axiom of choice) for $n$ when $n \in S$, and $0$ otherwise. For every $n \in S$ the chosen value satisfies $1 \le f(n)$, $2f(n) \le n$ and $\binom{n}{f(n)} = a$, by the specification of the choice.

## Every chosen k lies in [1, log2 a] {steps: hmaps}

Let $n \in S$ and $k = f(n)$. Then $1 \le k$, and
$$2^k \le \binom{2k}{k} \le \binom{n}{k} = a,$$
by the first helper, by monotonicity of $\binom{\cdot}{k}$ in the top index (as $2k \le n$), and by the choice of $k$. Since $2^k \le a$, $k \le \lfloor \log_2 a \rfloor$ (`Nat.le_log_of_pow_le`). So $f$ maps $S$ into $\{1, \dots, \lfloor \log_2 a \rfloor\}$.

## Different rows have different k {steps: hinj}

Let $n, m \in S$ with $f(n) = f(m) = k$, and suppose $n \ne m$. Then $\binom{n}{k} = \binom{m}{k} = a$, and $k \le n$, $k \le m$. If $n < m$ the second helper gives $\binom{n}{k} < \binom{m}{k}$, and if $m < n$ it gives $\binom{m}{k} < \binom{n}{k}$; either contradicts the equality. So $f$ is injective on $S$.

## Counting {steps: s4 s5}

An injective map from $S$ into the finite set $\{1, \dots, \lfloor \log_2 a \rfloor\}$ gives $\# S \le \#\{1, \dots, \lfloor \log_2 a \rfloor\} = \lfloor \log_2 a \rfloor$ (`Set.ncard_le_ncard_of_injOn`, then the cardinality of the interval).
