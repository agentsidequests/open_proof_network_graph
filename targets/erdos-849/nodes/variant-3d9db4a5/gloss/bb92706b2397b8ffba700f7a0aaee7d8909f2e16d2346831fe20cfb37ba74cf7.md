$$\Big(\forall t \ge 1,\ \exists a \in \mathbb{N},\ |R(a)| = t\Big) \;\Longrightarrow\; \exists a \in \mathbb{N},\ |R(a)| = 1, \qquad R(a) = \{\, n \in \mathbb{N} : \exists k \ge 1,\ 2k \le n,\ \tbinom{n}{k} = a \,\}.$$

The hypothesis is the root statement of Erdős 849 (every multiplicity $t \ge 1$ is attained by some $a$); the conclusion is this node's statement, the case $t = 1$. $|R(a)|$ is Lean's `Set.ncard`, which is $0$ on an infinite set.

The file proves the implication by specialising the hypothesis at $t = 1$, the side condition $1 \ge 1$ checked by `norm_num`. The file's first line labels the relation `partial`.
