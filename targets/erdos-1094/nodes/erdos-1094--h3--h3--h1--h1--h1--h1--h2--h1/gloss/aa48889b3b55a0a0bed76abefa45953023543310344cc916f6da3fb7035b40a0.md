$$f\ge0,\ F(s)-\frac{A}{s-1}\text{ continuous on }\Re s\ge1,\ g\text{ continuous},\ |g(u)|\le\frac{C}{1+u^2},\ \operatorname{supp}\hat g\text{ compact} \Rightarrow \sum_n \frac{f(n)}{n}\,g\bigl(\log\tfrac nX\bigr)\to A\int g$$

The Fourier-side form of the Wiener–Ikehara theorem (Korevaar, "A century of complex Tauberian theory", §III): for a continuous band-limited kernel g with quadratic decay, every weighted sum converges absolutely and tends to A ∫ g.
