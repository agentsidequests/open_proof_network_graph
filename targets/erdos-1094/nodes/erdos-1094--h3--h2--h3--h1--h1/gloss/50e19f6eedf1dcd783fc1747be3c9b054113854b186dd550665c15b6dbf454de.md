$$\exists c, K\ \forall k > K\ \exists S \subseteq \{p \text{ prime} : k < 2p,\ 16(2p-k)^4 \le k^3\}:\ k^3 < 16\,\big((|S| - 1)(2c\lfloor\log_2 k\rfloor + 1)\big)^4$$

A finite set $S$ of primes $p$ with $k/2 < p$ and $2p - k \le k^{3/4}/2$ whose size satisfies $(|S|-1)(2c\lfloor \log_2 k \rfloor + 1) > k^{3/4}/2$, in natural numbers.
