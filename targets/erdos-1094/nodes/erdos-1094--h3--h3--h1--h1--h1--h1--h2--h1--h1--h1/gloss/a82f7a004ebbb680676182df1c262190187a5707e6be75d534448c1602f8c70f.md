$$\exists K_0\ge0,\ c>0,\ K:\ K_0\ge c\text{ on }[-2,0],\ \forall X\ge1:\ \sum_n \frac{f(n)}{n}K_0\bigl(\log\tfrac nX\bigr)\text{ converges and is}\le K$$

The Wiener–Ikehara hypotheses bound the sums against one fixed nonnegative kernel.
