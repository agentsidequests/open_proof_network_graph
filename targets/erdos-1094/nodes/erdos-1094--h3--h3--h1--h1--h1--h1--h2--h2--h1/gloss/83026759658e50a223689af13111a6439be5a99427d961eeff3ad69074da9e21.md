$$\operatorname{supp}\psi\subseteq(0,\infty) \Rightarrow \int_{\mathbb R} e^u\psi(e^u)\,du=\int_{\mathbb R}\psi(x)\,dx$$

The substitution x = e^u maps ℝ onto (0, ∞), where ψ lives.
