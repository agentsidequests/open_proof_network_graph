$$h\text{ continuous},\ |h(u)|\le Me^u,\ h(u)=0\ (u\ge R),\ \varepsilon>0 \Rightarrow \exists g_1\le h\le g_2\text{ band-limited},\ |g_i(u)|\le\frac{C}{1+u^2},\ \int g_1\le\int h\le\int g_2,\ \int g_2-\int g_1\le\varepsilon$$

Step functions above and below h on a long interval, Selberg's majorants and minorants of interval indicators, and a majorant of the exponential tail of small integral.
