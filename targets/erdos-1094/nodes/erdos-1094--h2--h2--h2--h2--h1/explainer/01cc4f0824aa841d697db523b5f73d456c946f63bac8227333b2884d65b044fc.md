## Overview

Write $\theta=21/40$ and $\|x\|=|x-\mathrm{round}(x)|$. For $k\in\mathbb N$ and $n\in\mathbb Z$ let
$$B(k,n)=\bigl\{m\in\mathbb Z:\ k<m<k+k^{\theta},\ \|n/m\|<k^{\theta-1}\bigr\}$$
(in Lean, the integers of $(k,\,k+\lfloor k^{\theta}\rfloor+2)$ with these two properties). The claim: there is $k_0$ such that for $k\ge k_0$ and $\tfrac12k^{2-\theta}<n\le k^2/\log^2k$,
$$\#B(k,n)<g(k)=3+56\,k^{2\theta-1}+k^{\theta}(\log k)^{-2}.$$
The proof is an elementary count, carried out in lemmas before the theorem: sort the bad $m$ by the integer $h$ nearest to $n/m$; there are few possible $h$, and for each $h$ few $m$.

**Fibres.** Let $W(k)$ be the integers $m$ with $k<m<k+k^{\theta}$, and for $h\in\mathbb Z$ let $F(h)=\{m\in W(k):\ |n/m-h|<k^{\theta-1}\}$. Let $J=\mathbb Z\cap\bigl[\lfloor n/(k+k^{\theta})\rfloor,\ \lceil n/k\rceil\bigr]$.

**Every bad $m$ lies in a fibre over $J$** (for $k\ge 1$, $n>0$): take $h=\mathrm{round}(n/m)$; since $k<m<k+k^{\theta}$, $n/(k+k^{\theta})<n/m<n/k$, so $h\in J$, and $m\in F(h)$ by the definition of $B$.

**Few indices.** $\#J\le\lceil n/k\rceil-\lfloor n/(k+k^{\theta})\rfloor+1<\dfrac nk-\dfrac n{k+k^{\theta}}+3\le 3+\dfrac{n}{k^{2-\theta}}$, because $\dfrac nk-\dfrac n{k+k^\theta}=\dfrac{n k^{\theta}}{k(k+k^{\theta})}\le\dfrac{n k^{\theta}}{k^2}$.

**Small fibres.** Eventually in $k$ (once $k^{1-\theta}\ge 8$ and $k\ge 2$), for $n>\tfrac12k^{2-\theta}$ and every $h$, $\#F(h)\le 1+8k^{1+\theta}/n$. If $F(h)$ is empty this is clear. Otherwise pick $m_0\in F(h)$: then $h>n/m_0-1>n/(k+k^{\theta})-1\ge n/(2k)$, the last step because $n/(k+k^\theta)-n/(2k)\ge 1$ follows from $k^{2-\theta}\ge 8k$ and $k^\theta\le k$. For $m\in F(h)$, $|m-n/h|=\tfrac mh\,|n/m-h|$, with $m\le 2k$ and $h\ge n/(2k)$ giving $m/h\le 4k^2/n$; hence $|m-n/h|<4k^2/n\cdot k^{\theta-1}=4k^{1+\theta}/n$. An interval of half-length $L$ contains at most $2L+1$ integers, so $\#F(h)\le 1+8k^{1+\theta}/n$.

**The count.** Combining, for $k\ge\max(k_0',2)$ and $n$ in the range,
$$\#B(k,n)\le\sum_{h\in J}\#F(h)\le\Bigl(3+\frac n{k^{2-\theta}}\Bigr)\Bigl(1+\frac{8k^{1+\theta}}n\Bigr)=3+\frac{24k^{1+\theta}}n+\frac n{k^{2-\theta}}+8k^{2\theta-1}.$$
Now $n>\tfrac12k^{2-\theta}$ gives $k^{1+\theta}/n<2k^{2\theta-1}$, and $n\le k^2/\log^2k$ gives $n/k^{2-\theta}\le k^{\theta}(\log k)^{-2}$. So $\#B(k,n)<3+56k^{2\theta-1}+k^{\theta}(\log k)^{-2}=g(k)$.

## The bound for the lemma's set {steps: s1 h}

Take $k_0$ from the counting lemma; for $k\ge k_0$ and $n$ in the range it gives $\#B(k,n)<g(k)$.

## The statement's set is the same set {steps: hset close}

The set in the statement repeats the condition $k<m$ inside the filter, which already holds on $(k,\,k+\lfloor k^{\theta}\rfloor+2)$; with $\theta=21/40$ unfolded, the two filtered sets coincide, and their cardinalities satisfy the same bound.
