$$g\text{ continuous},\ |g(u)|\le\frac{D}{1+u^2} \Rightarrow \int_{-\log X}^{\infty} g\to\int_{\mathbb R} g$$

g is integrable, and the sets (−log X, ∞) increase to ℝ.
