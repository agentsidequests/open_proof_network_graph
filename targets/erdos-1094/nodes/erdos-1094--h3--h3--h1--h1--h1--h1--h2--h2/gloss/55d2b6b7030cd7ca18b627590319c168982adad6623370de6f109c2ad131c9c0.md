$$\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists g_1,g_2\text{ band-limited},\ |g_i(u)|\le\frac{C}{1+u^2},\ g_1(u)\le e^u\psi(e^u)\le g_2(u),\ \int g_1\le\int\psi\le\int g_2,\ \int g_2-\int g_1\le\varepsilon$$

Beurling–Selberg majorants and minorants (Vaaler, Bull. AMS 12 (1985)) of h(u) = e^u ψ(e^u), which is continuous, vanishes for large u and is O(e^u) as u → -∞; the integral bounds come from ∫ h = ∫ ψ.
