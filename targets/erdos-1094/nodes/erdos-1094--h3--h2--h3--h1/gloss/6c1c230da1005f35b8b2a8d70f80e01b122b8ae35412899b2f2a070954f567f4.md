$$\exists c, K\ \forall k > K\ \exists p, q \text{ prime}:\ k < 2p,\ p < q,\ 16\,(2q - k)^4 \le k^3,\ q - p \le c \log_2 k$$

Two primes in $(k/2,\ k/2 + k^{3/4}/2]$ whose difference is at most $c \lfloor \log_2 k \rfloor$; the bound $16(2q-k)^4 \le k^3$ is $2q - k \le k^{3/4}/2$ in natural numbers.
