$$f\ge0,\ \sum_{n<N} f(n)\le CN,\ |g(u)|\le\frac{D}{1+u^2},\ X>0 \Rightarrow \sum_n \frac{f(n)}{n}\,g\bigl(\log\tfrac nX\bigr)\text{ converges absolutely}$$

Compare with f(n)/(n(1+log² n)) and sum over dyadic blocks [2^j, 2^(j+1)).
