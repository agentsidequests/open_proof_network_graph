$$f\ge0,\ F(s)-\frac{A}{s-1}\text{ continuous on }\Re s\ge1 \Rightarrow \exists C\ \forall N:\ \sum_{n<N} f(n)\le C N$$

The partial sums of f grow at most linearly.
