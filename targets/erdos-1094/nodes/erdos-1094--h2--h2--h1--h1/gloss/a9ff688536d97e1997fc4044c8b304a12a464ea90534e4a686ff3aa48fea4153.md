$$\exists c>0\ \exists k_0\ \forall k\ge k_0:\ c\,k^{21/40}\le\psi\bigl(k+k^{21/40}\bigr)-\psi(k)$$

Here ψ is Mathlib's Chebyshev function, the sum of the von Mangoldt function over n ≤ x.
