$$\int_{\mathbb R}\hat g(\xi)\,e^{-2\pi i\xi\log X}\,G(1-2\pi i\xi)\,d\xi\to0 \text{ as } X\to\infty$$

The Riemann–Lebesgue lemma for ξ ↦ ĝ(ξ) G(1 − 2πiξ), evaluated at log X → ∞.
